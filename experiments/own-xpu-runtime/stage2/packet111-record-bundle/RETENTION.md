# Packet111 retained evidence

The local source copy, immutable parent snapshots, compiler receipts and four-view
CPU evidence are required to reproduce this binary. Restore tracked bytes with
Git; generated builds use build_engine.py. Immutable inherited objects and K
fixtures are linked without modifying their source. The build budget is 250 MB.
Original copied parent closeout and retention notes are in lineage-inputs.
No raw model weights or device results are included in this preparation.

The two tracked files over 1 MiB are deliberate: `rope.bf16` (4,194,304 bytes)
is the exact rotary table consumed by the native oracle/runtime, and
`lineage-inputs/packet103-composition-selection.json` (1,993,365 bytes) preserves
the fixed parent decision without pinning mutable parent outputs. Both restore
byte-for-byte from Git. Their consumers and hashes are in the copied lineage,
startup receipts and seals. Build products remain ignored; do not delete raw
failed-attempt evidence or replace a snapshot with a live parent reference.
