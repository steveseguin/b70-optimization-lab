# Packet209 admission and lifecycle authority

## Question and identity

Can the unchanged packet208 native image receive one narrowly admitted synthetic
qualification on BDF `0000:27:00.0` (historical physical index1), while the daily applications
remain unchanged on their own cards? The experiment is exactness only. It has no
throughput or model-quality claim and no endpoint stop/restore workflow.

## Outcome

CPU preparation passes 25 injected checks. This document and its source
pins are not a live manifest or permission to enumerate a device or launch the
qualification. The retained [root disposition](evidence/root-admission-disposition.json)
refuses live admission: vision PID3487303/start63453537 holds target
`card4/renderD131` and another card. All four cards have holders. Historical
physical indices do not determine current Linux DRM node names. No native
executable, including its static `--guard-check` mode, was invoked by these tests;
`runner.py --run` remains deliberately disabled.
Actual root admission must revalidate the current boot, fault baseline, physical
target ownership, source/image identity, resource forecast and unchanged daily
application identities immediately before launch. Earlier passive observations
are preparation evidence only.

## What worked

[authority-inputs.json](authority-inputs.json) binds existing owner, lane and drain
protocols. Target BDF27 belongs to cooperative laneA. The exactness reservation
does not reserve the entire host. Both queue PAUSE markers and their bytes must
stay unchanged and armed; STOP markers must be absent. Competing experiment
workers must remain absent. The daily applications are explicitly admitted by
PID/start-time and target-device identity; their continued presence is not an
idle-host or performance condition.

The selected native mask must name the exact target BDF and expose only ordinal0
inside the one child. Historical `native_env('A')` forces BDF23 and is unsuitable;
the two-card lane-A foreign-client gate would also reject the permitted card0
application. The new adapter must use exact target topology and raw privileged
passive ownership metadata, rejecting unknown or inaccessible evidence. The
existing inventory's global `passed=false` when daily applications hold other
cards is not itself a target-idle conclusion. HOME remains unchanged. Source and
environment admission must not introduce secret or ambient loader overrides.

Normal completion requires both shapes to match native p33 and the independent
CPU oracle, a safe native owner receipt, child exit0, target physical drain,
unchanged daily application identities and durable final evidence. Only then may
the owned drain permit and reservation complete. The new entry uses the unchanged
packet208 object/device image; any relabelled entry symbol must leave that image
byte-identical and remain bound to its original source.

## What failed or remains uncertain

No device result is claimed during preparation. Existing full-model host-memory
floors cannot be silently transferred to this small synthetic fixture while
applications remain active; its admitted forecast and growth limits must be
explicit. Native driver/compiler allocations remain outside tensor-size counts.

`Reservation.__exit__` and `DrainPermit.__exit__` always close their flocks.
Durable incomplete markers therefore do not establish that locks remain held.
Any uncertain post-Popen path must remain inside the held lifetime until child
exit, safe owner termination and target drain are established. An observed error
halts new native operations and forbids retry; bounded passive diagnostics may
continue. At most one cooperative child SIGTERM is allowed. Original errors
survive secondary journal, process-status, signal or evidence failures.

A failed but proven-drained run may unwind locks while leaving failed markers;
it must not call successful completion or remove those markers. An uncertain
owner or drain retains the locks and resources for coordinator review. No kill,
restart loop, automatic health retry, service mutation or power/memory-setting
change is part of this packet.
