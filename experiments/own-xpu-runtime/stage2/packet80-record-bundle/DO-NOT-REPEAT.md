# Packet80 boundaries

- Packed dense and experts2 variants are not registered: the brief fixes dense
  to production and experts to wide64. Do not extract HC from an experts2
  combined winner and borrow its confirmation.
- Lane-A wins or missing B results are insufficient. Every selected source
  winner must be 13/13 exact and faster under its own same-lane comparison.
- CPU proof is not GPU correctness, a speed result, or a record submission.
- Rank0 placement 0 passes split24 explicitly; split0 means historical22/26.
- The ordered queue capability stays rank1-only and preserves error/teardown.
- Legacy K2 measures inherited operators. New keys must survive the actual K2
  postprocessor without claiming new kernels were measured by old fixtures.
- Existing packets, screen wrappers and ops directories are frozen.
