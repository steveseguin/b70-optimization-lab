private include closure `native-pinned/` (44 files copied from packet58-onecard-cache23's generated header and frozen
one-card tree; provenance in `native-provenance.json`), `lineage-inputs/` (the 384-route JSON, identical to
packet192/193's derivation, and the parent provenance records), `proposals/`, the evidence receipts (`build.json`,
`isa.json` + `isa/*.asm.gz` (2 kernels, ~0.3 MB), `fixtures.json`, `cpu-rehearsal.json`, `admission.json`, `cpu-seal.json`,
`package-rows.json`, `check-queue-rows.json`, `pin-guard.json`, `plan-B.json`, `entry-report.json`, `final-checks.json`,
`final-pin-guard.json`, the two CPU host streams (~0.6 MB each), `do-not-repeat-search.txt`, the analyzer test log) and
`READY-FOR-DEVICE.json`.

Ignored (`.gitignore`): `build/` (three binaries ~0.6 MB; the 10 MB exactness fixtures under `build/fixtures/`; the
offline ISA pipeline intermediates under `build/isa/` ~20 MB; dependency lists; logs). Fixtures are regenerated byte for
byte by `rehearse_cpu.py` (GNU writes, Intel verifies); their digests are sealed in `evidence/fixtures.json`.

Parent inputs are read in place, never copied: `packet100b-route-capture/evidence/routes.bin` + `route-inputs.json`,
`packet162-iq2-lookup/build/micro-inputs/layer{0,24}.bin` (2 × 10,496,000 B of real GGUF rows, re-laid out at run
time), packet193's `diet-B1/samples.jsonl` (the two-card stream: refused by the analyzer; its real NaN-payload row is a
test input) and `DO-NOT-REPEAT.md` (the two-card datum), packet58-onecard-cache23's generated sources, `lineage/
path-rewrite.json` and `evidence/build.json` (record engine identity), packet58-onecard-diet's `layout-mismatch.json`.
Every `run_micro.py --plan/--run` re-hashes them against `lineage-inputs/parent-inputs.json` and `native-provenance.json`.

Device output: `evidence/dietmicro-B1/` (must not exist before the window): `samples.jsonl` (about 15 MB; 96 MiB
reserved), `window.json`, `analysis.json`, `s58dm_report.md`, kernel journals, `native-home/` (ignored), and
`active-lane-B.archived.json` only if a self-owned marker survived the release. The report appends one measured row to
`DO-NOT-REPEAT.md`. No retry: a failed window keeps its directory for review and the packet is not resealed.

Disk at preparation: root free ≈ 29 GB throughout (floor 27 GB for CPU work, 25 GB shared device floor); build tree
≈ 21 MB (cap 2 GB). Nothing was deleted to make room.
