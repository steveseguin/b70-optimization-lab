## Question and identity

Prepare record bundle 5 from committed packet97e with packet100 tile16 DOWN and
packet101 split32 draft attention. Keep the iq3-p49-split32-v1 oracle, two-card
rank1 draft placement, 24/24 target layers and full 16-bit KV. CPU only.

## Outcome

The requested CPU preparation passes. Both binaries compile and all 512 factorized
operator products pass. All 1,024 real-size rank startup/allocation checks pass,
as do all 5,632 finalized-source startup-window checks and 600 selected-path
consumer tests. Eight views are sealed. Both three-job composite proposals pass
temporary queue planning; inputs, storage admission and the fresh-chain plan
also pass. The build is below 300 MB. Composite device results and chain
readiness remain pending. No device result or speed improvement is claimed.

## What worked

The runtime selects every registered dimension in one binary per compiler.
Tile16 preserves the 360-byte DOWN row extent. Actual initialization/capture
checks cover new down dispatch, draft attention and all other choices together.
KD projects only unused target choices while preserving selected draft kernels.
Immutable parent metadata is copied locally. The submission fix and placement
and new-dimension disclosure are present before sealing.

## What failed or remains uncertain

The inherited K2 regression retained over 1 GB of temporary copies across 512
cases. It now removes each case after checking it. The first startup pass’s 357
successful compositions are retained as diagnostic history; a full repeat binds
the corrected sources. A later authentication interruption stopped at 164
compositions; its log is also retained. The final complete repeat binds unchanged
sources. Generic sealing then refused external fixture/manifest paths; seven
hash-checked fixture hard links and one local manifest snapshot repair the
metadata closure without changing runtime sources or the original oracle.
Every composite validation rechecks the original startup inputs. Draft split32
matches its own fixed reference; it is not
a claim of parity with the old draft reduction. Full target-model equality,
acceptance, repeats and speed remain device gates. A conservative disk refusal
was recorded and must pass before launch.

The requested six composite jobs pass temporary planning. The additional
conditional leave-one-out batch exceeds the inherited 240-second CPU planner
timeout; no conditional proposal is published. Its failed log remains in the
orchestration folder. Smaller temporary batches are needed if later measured
composite results require those jobs. This does not invalidate the completed
composite handoff or claim that the broader helper completed successfully.
