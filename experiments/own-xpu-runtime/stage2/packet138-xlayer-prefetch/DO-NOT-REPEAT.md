# Packet138 boundaries

- Pending real capture is neither a prediction result nor a failed lever.
- Do not use benchmark prompts to fit this predictor or count the sanity prompt.
- Do not flatten the four residual streams into one unnamed hidden vector.
- Rank rounded routing probabilities with the model's tie rule, not raw logits.
- Late residual prediction cannot consume time from before the residual exists.
- Do not count wrong or late prefetches as free, evict a live reader's slot,
  expose a partially filled slot, or double-spend bandwidth with 58v cache fills.
- 58g/58p's unprofiled zero duration fields are missing timing, not zero latency.
- Do not compare an optimistic simulated prefetch rate with a pessimistic 58v
  control; both arms use identical timings, initial cache and pointer rules.
- Keep packet58c/58d lifetimes, 58h/58i closures and 58n/58r/58t evidence intact.
- No phase-2 device design before the +3% and causal-traffic gates pass.
