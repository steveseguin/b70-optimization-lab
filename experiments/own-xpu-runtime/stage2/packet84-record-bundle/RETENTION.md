# Retention and rebuild

This local research bundle inherits hash-verified packet80 objects and retains
its source delta. Rebuild serially using build_engine.py, then full_shape.py,
real_head_gate.py, operator_matrix.py, retirement_gate.py, protocol_gate.py,
mini_gate.py, memory_matrix.py and prepare_chain.py. All CPU work uses nice19,
taskset8-15,24-31, python3 -B, OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 and stdin /dev/null.

Build products are local and ignored; keep under1.5GB. Every full-shape census
and operation capture is compressed under its content hash after exact roundtrip
verification; many compositions share identical allocations. Operator transcripts
are similarly retained once by hash. Large matrix summaries are required evidence
for all1,344 admitted configurations and are regenerated by the scripts above.
No historical evidence is removed. This is not a published standalone recipe.

Source seals bind the shared v4-lanes wrapper unchanged. Cached proof excludes
orchestration helpers; ready.json separately binds current helpers, source screen
receipts, selected seal and selected dry run. A pin change recomputes that cheap
selection receipt. No unchanged cached proof is relabeled as a device result.
