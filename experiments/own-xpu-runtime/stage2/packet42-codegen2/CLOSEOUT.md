## Question and identity

Prepare launch-floor measurements and a second generated-code study for the
quantized Qwen3.8 Flash-Next runtime on one Arc Pro B70. Arithmetic identity v2,
packet29 reference core and packet37 selected incumbent; October 12, 2026.
This packet is CPU-only preparation. No device program or discovery ran.

## Outcome

Offline candidates, CPU gates and coordinator windows are prepared. No speed,
cheapest geometry, device byte agreement or dispatch promotion is established.
Those questions remain for the coordinator in K0, T1 and K2 order.

## What worked

Conditional expert CFG counting exposes the specific messages changed by packed
loads and table access. Parallel route rank removes a stage without a serial
512-item lane loop. The launch-floor matrix distinguishes geometry, arguments,
local accessor, barriers and split-launch overhead with the runtime event path.
CPU-only mock windows exercise receipt handling through the real summaries.

## What failed or remains uncertain

Counts cannot prove why select beat bank; dependency and occupancy explanations
are hypotheses. A no-finish split4 form with independent subgroups cannot obey
both the original part ownership and the ban on new synchronization; the prepared
alternative gives a whole row to one subgroup. Nonfinite GEMV retains the old
unqualified reference differences. CPU gates do not execute device vector loads.
Only device timing can establish whether candidates help.
