# Packet58e result and submission boundary

This is the one-card plain-decode lineage for the quantized compressed
Unsloth UD-IQ3_XXS version. Arithmetic, oracle, BF16 KV and 128-token scope
remain unchanged. No two-card record threshold or publication exception transfers.

The seven-window chain provides exact repeats and one whole-host timing window.
WS128x may share the host with lane B: its timing is diagnostic, not a second
speed qualification. `packet58_submission.py` marks that distinction and
refuses payload creation without a separately qualified whole-host repeat.
No external submission is prepared or sent by this CPU task.

The strict 512-token gate remains false. Standard 512-token prefill and full
natural completion are not measured. Full device evidence, repeat timing and
the one-card publication scope must qualify before any external record claim.
