# Retention and reconstruction

Existing packets, screen and ops trees are frozen. The only new ops path is
`proposed-p128b-attr-B.txt`. `parent-manifest.json` pins packet128's original
source/configuration bytes; `copy-symlinks.json` records all 84 preserved links.
Original scratch, receipts and the intrusive device run stay in packet128.
New evidence does not reuse their qualification flags.

The ignored build is rebuilt by README's CPU sequence. It links exact inherited
arithmetic objects, records their hashes and rebuilds the instrumented engine
entry. `instrumentation.patch` records the sampling and queue-route change against frozen packet128.
The arithmetic objects retain their separate packet124b provenance.
Generated binaries and build scratch are never committed as source.

`rope.bf16` exceeds 1 MiB and is the inherited deterministic numerical constant
table needed by the exact engine data closure. Preserve its bytes; restore from
packet128 at the hash in `parent-manifest.json`. No weights are committed.
Generated Python caches are excluded. Retain future event/native receipts in
`evidence/device-B`; the compact budget and note should carry their hashes.

`parent-reanalysis.json` is CPU reanalysis of packet128's old device receipts,
with inferred route labels and sampling applied after recording. It is retained
only to reproduce the old analyzer failure's correction and failed overhead
gate, never as packet128b device evidence.
