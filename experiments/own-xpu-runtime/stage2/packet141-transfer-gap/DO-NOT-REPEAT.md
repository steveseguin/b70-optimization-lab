# Packet141 boundaries

- Do not call this single captured half a model throughput benchmark.
- Do not sum medians of events, split fused kernel time or omit zero-launch sites
  from the coverage manifest. Sum durations within each complete sample first.
- Never replace actual routes with timed easy routes; all 24 layers must match
  the pinned packet138 capture, and every generated token must match packet49.
- No device run before the CPU seal/argument/allocation/queue gates pass.
- No graph-event profiling or external tracing. Prepared native calls here are
  ordinary queue submissions and event clocks are read after completion.
- Restoring state is diagnostic replay only. It earns no headline or quality
  exemption. Pre-touch time is retained, not a free speedup.
- Do not interpret the kill gate if the forced-cold condition disagrees with
  natural sequence timing by >10% in a class costing >=0.5 ms/half.
- Device evidence is pending. This preparation does not close the lane or alter
  the record, existing packets, services or queues.
- Original enqueue/finish is required, including the uninstrumented wall control.
  Withhold a verdict on >10% observer overhead or >=0.5 ms and >10% issue gaps.
- No-extra-eviction follows restoration; it does not prove engine cache occupancy.
