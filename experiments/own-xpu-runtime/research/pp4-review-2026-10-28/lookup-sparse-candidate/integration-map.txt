Materiality falsifier
Wholecopy startup29.14GB is real capacity work, but chunk replacement cannot accelerate existing anonymous-memory row reads by itself: it adds sorting/binary search/copy. Its possible gain is eliminating fullcopy startup/anonymous capacity while charging fresh demand I/O into each chunk. Existing mapped long result lost91.79s stage0 request time; source alone cannot assign its cause. Chunk sorting coalesces only adjacent chosen rows, not general sparse pages, and generated misses still expose mapped backing costs. Existing OWNRT_PREFILL_PROFILE starts AFTER prefill_lookups, so it cannot rank lookup against experts. Smallest useful attribution is a bounded wall+fault counter around prefill_lookups (stage/copy/decode/upload split if warranted), charged to request; no new device profiling required. Without that measurement plus exact gate/fair lifecycle timing, prioritize measured expert/kernel bottlenecks rather than promote sparse as a speed win. Sparse remains a small concrete candidate if startup/capacity is the selected objective, never a speculative performance claim.
