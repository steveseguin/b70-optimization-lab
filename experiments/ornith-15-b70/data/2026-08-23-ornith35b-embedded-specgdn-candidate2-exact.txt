# Why Deterministic Validation Matters for GPU Inference Kernels

When optimizing GPU kernels, determinism in your validation process isn't just a nice-to-have—it's foundational to trusting your optimizations. Here's why:

## 1. Isolating Correctness from Variance

GPU execution involves inherently non-deterministic elements:
- **Thread scheduling** and ordering in reductions/atomics
- **Floating-point associativity** (a+b+c ≠ c+b+a in general)
- **Non-deterministic kernels** from libraries like cuBLAS
