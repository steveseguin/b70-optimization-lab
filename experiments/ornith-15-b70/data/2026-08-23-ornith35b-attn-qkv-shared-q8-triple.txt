# Why Deterministic Validation Matters for GPU Inference Kernels

When optimizing GPU kernels, determinism in validation isn't just a nice-to-have—it's foundational to producing trustworthy results. Here's why it matters:

## 1. Isolating Correctness from Performance

GPU optimizations introduce many sources of non-determinism:
- **Floating-point reordering** — parallel reduction order changes summation results
- **Non-deterministic kernel launch configurations** — thread block scheduling varies
- **Atomic operations** — race conditions produce different accumulation orders
