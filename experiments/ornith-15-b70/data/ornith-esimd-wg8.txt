# Why Deterministic Validation Matters for GPU Inference Kernels

When optimizing GPU kernels, determinism in validation isn't just a nice-to-have—it's foundational to trusting your optimizations. Here's why:

## 1. Isolating Correctness from Optimizations

GPU optimizations introduce many sources of non-determinism:
- **Floating-point reordering** — parallel reductions sum elements in different orders, producing slightly different results
- **Non-deterministic kernel launch order** — when multiple kernels write to the same memory
- **Atomic operation ordering** — race conditions in atomic adds
