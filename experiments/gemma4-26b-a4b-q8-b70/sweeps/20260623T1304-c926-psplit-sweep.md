draft-MTP n=7, `n_min=2`, `p_min=0.12`, no draft backend sampling, 32 draft

## Decision

Loss. `MTP_P_SPLIT=0.05-0.20` is not a record path on the current
`c926ad098` + `ctx-checkpoints 0` identity. The best p-split mean
(`90.171969`) is about `0.985 tok/s` below the promoted `91.156717` record.

Keep the data as a valid negative result. Next higher-leverage path is a
source-level draft-MTP sampler test: expose the hardcoded draft sampler
`top_k=10` as a gated environment variable and test `top_k=1/2/4` under the
record identity. That directly targets the measured draft sampler overhead
without changing target sampling or main model quality.
