## Decision

Negative. Do not promote or submit. The `single` mode is valid after the assert
fix, but it is slower than the paired controls and does not crack 100 tok/s.

Interpretation: the positive p021 small-ncols win appears specific to
multi-column verifier shapes (`ne[1]` 2..8). For the single-column shape, the
existing specialized path remains better. Future work should return to the
current hot nodes under a fresh node profile instead of extending this p021 path
further.
