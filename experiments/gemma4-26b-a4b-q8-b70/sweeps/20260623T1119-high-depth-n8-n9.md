## Hypothesis

The current best filled-long lane uses `--spec-draft-n-max 7`, strong
acceptance, and a 588/512 benchmark shape. A prior `n=8` run used
`p-min=0.15` and collapsed, but that did not rule out `n=8` with the lower
confidence gates that work for `n=7`. This sweep tested whether more draft
depth could break the `91.05 tok/s` high-water mark without lowering quality.

## Decision

Loss. All four runs preserve quality at full promotion-depth canary, but they
are far below the current valid record (`91.05 tok/s`). The `n=8` and `n=9`
frontier is rejected for the current `dec5ca557` llama.cpp AOT runtime and
filled-long shape. Future high-depth work needs a source/runtime mechanism
change, not more `p-min` neighbors.
