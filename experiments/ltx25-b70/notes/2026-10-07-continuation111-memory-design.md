# Native111 continuation memory admission design

Status: source-only, unimplemented and unmeasured. Use successful110 as the numerical/resource predecessor; its decode measurements do not establish image-encoding safety. A bounded first conditioned request appears feasible with unchanged native guards, after the exact safety and admission changes below. This is not authorization to launch or a guarantee against OOM.

## Actual encode path and lifetime

`LTXVImgToVideoInplace.execute` in sealed110 `comfy_extras/nodes_lt.py:152` clones the incoming video latent, derives spatial dimensions from that latent and the VAE's32× scale, bilinearly resizes the full-float anchor if necessary, then calls the supplied VAE's `encode`. It writes the encoded prefix and sets its noise mask at strength1. StageA therefore encodes one320×192 frame; stageB encodes the original one640×384 anchor again after upsampling. The stageA encoded latent cannot be reused for stageB: the resolutions and native computations differ. The upsampler still removes the mask at `nodes_lt_upsampler.py:62`.

The supplied object is the already resident video `CausalDiffusionVAE` on GPU3, sharing that card with audio VAE and the text-secondary shard. `native_adapter._discover` binds this exact object and patcher. Its constructor selects BF16 and `disable_offload=True` (`sd.py:634–650`), including both encoder and decoder weights. There is no new model/replica to load for conditioning. Full residence, ownership, source and dtype checks must still verify that claim in the actual111 process.

`VAE.encode` (`sd.py:1418`) reshapes the one-frame F32 image into[1,3,1,H,W], obtains a workspace estimate, invokes normal full-load admission, normalizes input and converts to the existing BF16 VAE compute dtype. This class has no chunked-I/O flag, so the input moves to `self.device` before `first_stage_model.encode`. `CausalDiffusionVAE.encode` (`na_diffusion_decoder.py:511`) encodes, splits means/logvar and normalizes means; it does not sample a posterior RNG. `Encoder.forward_orig` has exactly its leading-frame chunk for T=1. Its `forward` finally removes calling-thread temporal-cache entries, including on exceptions (`causal_video_autoencoder.py:285–323`). Preserve this cleanup and the original exception flow.

The sealed launcher uses CPU/F32 intermediates (no GPU-only or FP16-intermediate option). Empty stageA latents and upsampled stageB latents are CPU intermediates; encoded output returns there too. Native conditioning clones215,040/860,160-byte F32 video latents and writes30,720/122,880-byte encoded prefixes at stagesA/B. The shared F32 anchor is2,949,120 bytes. These payload sizes are not total CPU allocation peaks: resizing, normalization, serialization and graph retention hold other buffers. Keep the anchor owned and immutable through both conditioning calls and full request completion. Keep latent clones/masks until their native graph consumers finish; do not add alias-based reuse, manually empty allocator caches or truncate graph outputs. VAE weights remain resident between calls; transient encoder tensors and caches must not become cross-chunk state.

## Existing guards and concrete missing safety

`NativeReferenceSafety.before` requires8/8/2/9GiB physical free across GPU0..3, strict source/object/residency identity, no graph sampler routes or decoder replicas, and the trusted native phase. Its post-request floor is2GiB/card. `NativeAdapter.before_request` wraps the entire executor in `protect_residence`, which refuses loader pressure before eviction and protects all loaded owners. Leave both unchanged.

**Blocking implementation gap:** `native_safety.transform_sd` currently hooks only `VAE.decode`. The `VAE.encode` exception handler at `sd.py:1450` still calls `raise_non_oom`, warns, sets `do_tile=True`, then calls `soft_empty_cache` and a tiled encoder. A continuation OOM would therefore enter an unqualified retry/cache path. Add an exact source-pinned insertion immediately after `model_management.raise_non_oom(e)` in the regular-encoding handler: when the existing safety attribute is attached, call its `reject_oom(self,e)` before the warning or `do_tile=True`. Preserve the decode insertion. Validate AST placement separately in `VAE.encode` and `VAE.decode`, one each, plus CPU exceptional-path tests proving no tile/cache call on OOM and unchanged non-OOM propagation. Do not make a broad global replacement or catch a refusal and continue.

## Proposed admission and barriers

1. Preserve the existing native preload equation,8/8/2/9GiB request admission, full residence, no-eviction guard, fault checks and2GiB post floor. Use one sequential request at a time; no sampler pipeline workers/replicas or concurrent VAE users. Validate bound VAE/device/dtype, CPU/F32 intermediate policy and exactly the two expected one-frame shapes.
2. Add request-owned conditioning-stage guards around the two actual native conditioning calls, with fixed A→B order and exactly one call per stage. Before each call, synchronize and re-inspect using the same8/8/2/9GiB requirements; do not reset the controller's active request, seen set or failure latch. Before encode, bind the actual input shape and source estimator result; keep the native loader's checks active. After each completed call, synchronize and require the same identity/full residence and2GiB/card floor before exposing output to the next graph consumer. Check encoder temporal caches are clear for the executing thread; no other owner/thread is admitted. Capture allocations/reserved/cumulative peak observations without resetting counters. These barriers add no tensor arithmetic or numerical reuse.
3. Persist source-bound per-stage before/after receipts with request, prompt, predecessor anchor hash, stage, actual shapes/dtypes/devices, loader estimate, physical free and peak observations. Save failure receipts too, including OOM/partial-stage evidence, then halt new requests. No tiled retry, automatic resend, reload loop or guard reduction.
4. Keep the first unconditioned chunk's existing49-frame header/memory barrier. After the **first conditioned chunk**, require a new durable barrier binding both stage receipt pairs, unchanged source/model/runtime, all four complete finite output tensors, predecessor linkage and post-request memory before chunk2 or replay proceeds. Do not let the unconditioned first-native barrier stand in for this check. Later exact replay must match all four tensors at every chunk and the predecessor anchors; seam quality and audio alignment remain separate gates.

## Feasibility, uncertainty and stop conditions

The pinned source estimate is80×max(T,7)×H×W×2 bytes:68,812,800 bytes (0.06409GiB) for stageA,275,251,200 bytes (0.25635GiB) for stageB. These are **estimates**, not measured encoder peaks. The launcher's2GiB reserve makes normal fully resident loader workspace admission max(2.8GiB,estimate+2GiB)=2.8GiB for either encode. Keep the actual loader equation authoritative; do not substitute this calculation for its checks or double-count resident weights as newly missing.

110's native before/after GPU3 readings remained at least14.485GiB, making the unchanged9GiB pre-encode floor plausible on the same placement. Requiring fresh readings immediately before both calls handles coexistence with stageA sampling/upsampling results; the second call cannot borrow the first reading. No additional preload reserve is justified solely by these small source estimates, and raising the global reserve arbitrarily could recreate108's impossible preload equation. Conversely, the estimate and historical free memory cannot prove a peak bound. The first conditioned request remains a controlled resource qualification, with fresh admission and fault-halt behavior, not a declaration of proven-safe maximum allocation.

Refuse on missing/changed encode hook, unexpected VAE/output policy, extra/misordered conditioning call, unavailable9GiB GPU3 pre-margin, any other native pre-floor failure, loader eviction demand, residual thread cache, nonfinite output, postfloor failure or fault. Preserve evidence and reassess ownership/placement/source causes; do not lower guards or rerun just to obtain a pass. Fresh CPU/disk admission also needs a finite six-output-plus-setup/anchor budget; no full110 archive scan is needed for this design.
