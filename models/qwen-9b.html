Qwen3.5 9B

Alibaba's dense-hybrid Qwen3.5 9B (linear-attention layers with a full-attention layer every fourth, and a shipped MTP head), served on one Arc Pro B70 as RedHatAI's FP8-dynamic checkpoint through the lab's deterministic vLLM XPU stack with lossless speculative decoding. Two routes are measured: RedHatAI's FP8-dynamic and its W4A16 INT4, the latter faster and byte-exact at every concurrency through 64 users.
