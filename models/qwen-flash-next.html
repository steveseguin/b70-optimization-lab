Qwen3.8 Flash-Next · 125B-A6B

Qwen's experimental 125B-A6B hybrid-attention MoE. Its 51B n-gram embedding enables selective host placement, and the official FP8 export now serves cache-zero text across the complete configured-512 MTP0-4 depth grid on four B70s. MTP4 reached a 20.727 tok/s short-screen decode median. At exact 4K, 32-block MTP1 and MTP2 recipes passed at 8.904 and 9.893 tok/s decode; MTP3 remains preferred at 15.502 tok/s decode, 187.9 seconds TTFT, and 1.246 tok/s wall output. Bounded MTP1 active-1K and active-2K arms both stopped on their first requests with no output; the 2K arm reached its 360-second client bound, the engine then reported its own timeout, and the post-failure teardown window recorded resets of both engine classes on all four cards before device rediscovery. MTP2 active-1K returned the exact frozen target twice with zero cache reuse and perfect two-position acceptance, but corrected local-NVMe events after the frozen cutoff failed its strict clean-host gate, so its 10.683 and 12.642 tok/s observations are diagnostic only and the cell remains quarantined. Separate MTP2 and MTP3 active-2K requests completed their generic exact-depth gates but diverged from the frozen MTP0 token array at generated tokens 13 and five; both diagnostic rates receive no speed or quality credit and those cells are quarantined. MTP4 remains quarantined at exact 4K. Deterministic direct-answer quality is 6/7 semantically, while the separate official-thinking target profile passed 25/25. The MTP3 transfer completed and matched MTP0 on 19 required rows before the repeated-session boundary stopped the remaining six, so MTP thinking parity at depth 3 and deployment stability remain inconclusive and unqualified.
