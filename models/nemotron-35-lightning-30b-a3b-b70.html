What to know

  • With reasoning ON, repeated identical requests are not hash-stable (thinking-channel sampling); deterministic with reasoning off - the recipe default.
  • MoE rate reflects ~3B active parameters.
  • 1M native context not exercised beyond 32K here.
  • Two-card layer split measured 69.45/69.49 tok/s vs 72.17/72.04 on one card (-3.7%): use one card for single-stream decode.
