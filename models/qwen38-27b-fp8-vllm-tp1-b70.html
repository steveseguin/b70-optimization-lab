Qwen3.8 27B official FP8 on one Intel Arc Pro B70

Official Qwen FP8 weights on one Intel Arc Pro B70. Recommended: MTP depth 5 with a draft-only INT4 shortlist head, 16,384 tokens of context, 53.5 tok/s writing speed; outputs identical to no MTP. A no-quantization profile (51.6 tok/s, 12,544 tokens) drafts with an FP16 copy of the output layer instead of an INT4 one.
