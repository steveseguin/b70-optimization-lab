neither the commit-timestamp problem nor the closure gaps were caught before

1. **Require a per-lane census script (or waiver note) before bisection, plus a `DO-NOT-REPEAT.md` per active lane.** Evidence: qwen35-9b's three-day, dozen-note elimination chase had neither. Expected saving: AGENTS.md records that rule 1 turned a similar three-day chase (qwen3.8-27b R64-R146) into one evening; same order of saving here. Generalizes to any future lane with a batch/shape-sensitive greedy output.

Expected saving: restores the audit's only latency signal so next week's report can say whether turnaround is improving. Generalizes to any lane whose notes are committed post-hoc as a batch.
