1. **Fix the non-recursive campaign glob** at
   `scripts/efficiency-audit-metrics.py:107` to also match
   `experiments/*/data/**/preregistration.json`, and add a second source
   pairing `notes/*-prereg*.md` with `notes/*-result*.md` by slug. Evidence:
   this week's glob caught 4 of 11 real prereg files and 0 of the
   `qwen38-27b-b70`/flash-next lanes' note-paired campaigns; 3 of the missed
   ones show `status: "passed"`, which the script's own outcome regex would
   classify correctly. Expected saving: "campaigns" becomes the real count
   every week. Generalizes to any lane using either convention.
2. **Land the 09-09 shallow-clone/`--as-of` fix.** At least the ninth
   report to hit it: this week's raw run reported 50 commits spanning 2.6
   hours as "7 days." Expected saving: removes the manual
   `git fetch --deepen` correction every audit redoes by hand. Generalizes
   to any read-only tool trusting `git log --since` in a fresh sandbox.
3. **Install `gh`, or make `release_assets()` fail loudly instead of
   returning empty.** `which gh` is empty; `tools/public-closure-scanner.py:59-65`
   turns that into "zero assets," so every `missing_release_asset` finding
   this week (8 on `qwen35-4b-w4a16-b70`, 6 on
   `qwen38-flash-next-fp8-tp4-mtp3-b70`, 2 on the new `...exactgdn` package)
   is unverifiable. Same class of bug as 09-12: `PATH_RE` truncates an
   f-string path at `{model}` in

   and the builder glob `repro/**/Dockerfile*` misses
   `repro/qwen35-9b-w4a16-b70/docker/r276-dynamic-mamba-alloc.Dockerfile`, a
   real, git-tracked, tagged builder. Expected saving: closure reports stop
   sending maintainers after gaps that don't exist. Generalizes to any check
   treating a missing dependency or naming assumption as a confirmed
   negative.
