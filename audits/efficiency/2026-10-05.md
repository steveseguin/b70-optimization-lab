## Verdict

The lab is fast once it is working and idle the rest of the time. `/tmp/eff.json` lists commits on only three of
the seven days (2026-10-03, -04, -05); the other four show no entry at all, i.e. zero commits lab-wide, then a
481-commit, three-day burst (103 / 362 / 16) with gaps between commits of at most 2.5 hours
(`longest_commit_gaps_hours`). Inside that burst, discipline is good: the script's automated
`single_server_speed_verdicts` and `frozen_oracle_gates` signal lists are both empty, census-before-build and
fast-fail-on-fault both show up in the notes, and a preregistered stopping rule was honoured even when a later arm
looked slightly better. The two real drags are process, not judgement: more than half of this week's commits are
one-per-pipeline-stage LTX receipts, and one finished LTX result is sitting on an owner decision that could have
been asked for hours earlier.

## Where the week went

Three stretches that did not close with an accepted result:

1. **LTX four-card host, packets 94d-96 (10-04 12:47-21:01, ~8h).** A chain of sampler-batching, worker-count and
   shared-pool arms. By 18:33 (`787f35040`) batch-2 was already known to be row-independent but not bit-identical
   to a solo clip ("Same mathematics, different rounding"). The chain kept building batch-4, four-card layouts and
   a shared pool for three more hours before `CURRENT.md`'s 20:40 entry stated "Decision needed from the owner."
   No listed rule tells a runner to raise a quality fork the moment it is found rather than after the sweep.
2. **qwen38-27b-b70, comm5 attempt 1 (10-03 22:36-22:42, 6 min).** Prereg `2026-10-03-fp8-comm5-prereg.md`, then a
   profiler tripped the memory guard, the kill logged fault lines, and the campaign halted
   (`experiments/qwen38-27b-b70/notes/2026-10-03-fp8-comm5-attempt1-guard-kill.md`). Rule 5 (fast-fail on faults)
   worked exactly as written; this is a short, well-handled stretch, not wasted time.
3. **LTX packet 92b, two contexts on one card (10-04 02:40-02:46 UTC, 6 min).** A decode-ahead child process on
   `xpu:3` next to the server hit a hard lockup and a CAT-error fault within 90 seconds
   (`experiments/ltx25-b70/notes/2026-10-04-packet-92b-two-contexts-fault.md`). The note says the cause ("VRAM
   pressure across contexts, the preceding interrupt lockups, or the driver's two-fault-mode-VM handling") is still
   not established. No existing rule covers two execution contexts sharing one card; this looks like new ground for
   the stability guide rather than a rule that was skipped.

## Top three changes

1. **Commit one entry per campaign arm, not one per pipeline stage.** Evidence: of this week's 481 commits, 279
   subjects end in "receipts" (`git log --since='7 days ago' --format='%s' | grep -c 'receipts$'`); one arm,
   `shard3-c-w3-b2-p1`, alone produced 14 separate receipt commits at 2026-10-04T21:01:35. Saving: this is the data
   this audit itself has to wade through every week; squashing stage receipts into one commit per arm cuts the
   window's commit count by over half with no evidence lost. Generalizes to any future lane whose runner writes a
   receipt file per pipeline stage.
2. **Flag a quality-judgment fork to the owner the moment it is found, not after the sweep.** Evidence: the
   LTX batch-2 rounding difference was known at 18:33 (`787f35040`) but the campaign kept extending for three more
   hours before the explicit ask landed in `CURRENT.md` at 20:40. Saving: the owner's ruling can run in parallel
   with the rest of the sweep instead of after it — hours on a fast lane, more on a slow one. Generalizes: this is
   the third time a lane has hit a precision fork (video decode 09-19, FP8 drafting kernel, now LTX batching), so
   it is a repo-wide habit to adopt, not an LTX fix.
3. **Give LTX campaigns the same accept/reject marker qwen38 campaigns get.** Evidence: `campaigns_by_lane_outcome`
   shows 18 of 21 counted LTX campaigns as "unclassified" against qwen38-27b-b70's 3 accepted / 3 unclassified / 4
   no-result-yet of 10, even though LTX packets do reach real conclusions (e.g. "batch 2 proven row-independent...
   135/135," `787f35040`) — there is just no file field the metrics script can read. This builds on triage row 3
   ("partly applied"): discovery now counts LTX packets correctly, so the open gap has narrowed to outcome
   classification specifically. Saving: removes a counting blind spot for every future weekly audit. Generalizes:
   any lane using summary.json receipts instead of prereg/result markdown needs the same one-line outcome field.
