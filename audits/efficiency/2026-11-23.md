## Top three changes

1. **Teach campaign discovery to recognize dated-notes lanes, not just prereg/summary.json.** Evidence: `campaigns_total=0` this week despite 60 own-xpu-runtime commits with explicit before/after numbers and gate results (`experiments/own-xpu-runtime/notes/*.md`, `experiments/own-xpu-runtime/inkling2/notes/inkling2-forward.md`). Expected saving: restores the lab's only automated view of campaign throughput and latency for its most active lane; without it every future week under this convention reports the same false zero. Generalizes because any new lane that documents arms in prose (as `inkling2` already does) will hit the identical blind spot.
2. **Start `experiments/own-xpu-runtime/inkling2/DO-NOT-REPEAT.md` now.** Evidence: the sibling `stage2/` tree grew to 300+ packet directories each with its own `DO-NOT-REPEAT.md`; inkling2 already has dead/neutral arms recorded only as prose in commit messages, with no durable negative-result ledger yet. Expected saving: a few minutes now avoids re-deriving results like the round19 "lost to the old exact step" finding for every future arm. Generalizes to every new engine lane: write the ledger at lane birth, not after sprawl.
3. **Record what happens during multi-day no-commit stretches.** Evidence: 129.3 of the week's 168 hours (77%) produced zero commits, explained only after the fact by the next commit's message. Expected saving: a one-line note at pause/resume (what's running, why no commits) turns the audit's "Where the week went" from reconstruction into a read, cutting review time for this and future weeks.
