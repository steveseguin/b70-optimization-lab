## Top three changes

1. **Make the metrics script detect and refuse a shallow clone**, exiting
   with an actionable error instead of misreporting. Evidence: this
   report's first draft, caught only by review. Saving: prevents every
   future audit from running against incomplete history. Generalizes to
   any script here reasoning over `git log`.
2. **Add an explicit repeat-infra-fault census rule alongside rule 1**, at
   the second occurrence of a crash/freeze signature. Evidence:
   `.../2026-09-20-freeze-1145-packet88-correction.md`'s "two campaigns
   spent on it." Saving: ~1 packet-cycle per repeat-fault incident.
   Generalizes rule 1's discipline from kernel bisection to hardware-fault
   diagnosis, this week's dominant failure mode.
3. **Keep authoring while a lane is fault-blocked, and maintain its
   DO-NOT-REPEAT.md.** Evidence: packets 91/92 undrafted after 5.1 idle
   days; no `ltx25-b70` DO-NOT-REPEAT.md despite 7 freeze notes. Saving:
   recovers idle days as design work and a fault index. Generalizes to any
   lane hitting a human-reboot gate.
