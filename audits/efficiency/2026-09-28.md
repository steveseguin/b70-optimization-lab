## Top three changes

1. **Update `CURRENT.md` at every campaign boundary, not only at incident boundaries.** Evidence:
   `CURRENT.md` (`f6f5cdbd`, 01:35 -04:00) says boot `f64b14c5` "waits on a reboot," but three later commits
   show a reboot happened and two more packets ran, unreflected in `CURRENT.md`. Saving: prevents the exact
   misreading this audit's first draft made, and any operator decision made from `CURRENT.md` alone during a
   silence. Generalizes: a sole-source-of-truth file is only true if refreshed at resumption, not just at halt.
2. **Distinguish a documented halt from a silent stop in `scripts/efficiency-audit-metrics.py`.** Evidence:
   this audit's first draft attributed the full 171-hour gap to the 09-20 halt note until a review comment
   showed campaigns had resumed twice more; the real unexplained gap starts at `b1c54c1c`. Saving: a
   machine-checkable "last activity vs. last documented reason for silence" field catches this without a
   second reviewer. Generalizes to every future idle-week audit.
3. **Spend idle GPU-blocked weeks on the publication-closure backlog, but fix the scanner's `gh` blind spot
   first.** Evidence: the scanner finds real, non-asset gaps (hardcoded host paths, missing paths, unreachable
   builders) in 12 of 28 candidate packages — GPU-free work untouched this week — but its
   `missing_release_asset` counts are unreliable since `gh` is absent, already flagged in the 2026-09-12 audit.
   Saving: closes a two-week-old recommendation, stops future audits citing inflated gap counts. Generalizes to
   any tool that turns a failed subprocess call into a confirmed negative.
