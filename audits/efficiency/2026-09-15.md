# Efficiency audit, 2026-09-15

## Verdict

The lab is fast at the bench and slow at the edges. 797 commits landed in
7 days, and `ltx25-b70` produced a bitwise-exact 1.83x sampler speedup
from per-block XPU graph capture (`sampler_speedup: 1.834`,
`data/graph-capture-19-result.json`) — real, but "one process," short of
Rule 3's two-fresh-server bar for a speed verdict. The real cost sits
where the measuring stick and publication pipeline both miss it: (1)
`efficiency-audit-metrics.py` "saw" only 4 of this week's campaigns
because most lanes now nest `preregistration.json` in a campaign
subdirectory instead of the flat `*-prereg.json` its glob expects, so the
busiest lane (`qwen38-27b-b70`, 109 commits) is invisible to it; and (2)
14 of 27 publication-tier packages have closure findings, some real
(missing release assets), some scanner artifacts. Net: faster at
optimizing, not yet faster at publishing or measuring its own throughput.

**Stretch #3 — qwen38-27b-b70, duration unknown.** 5+ new campaign

## Top three changes

1. **Match the measuring stick to the naming lanes actually use.** Extend `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` to also match `data/*/preregistration.json`, fix campaign-id extraction (`re.sub(r"-?prereg.*$", "", name)` empties on that filename), and check `summary.json` when no `*result*.json` exists. Evidence: 4 campaigns counted vs. 11+ new preregistration files this window. Saving: restores visibility into most real throughput. Generalizes to any lane using this directory convention.
2. **Teach the closure scanner container-build paths and cross-package builders before any template rewrite.** `/opt/vllm-src` is created by the Dockerfile itself; a "missing" builder for one tag exists in a sibling package's `build-image.sh`. Saving: avoids rewriting working templates over scanner artifacts. Generalizes to any multi-package lane sharing templates.
3. **Upload the 8 missing qwen38-27b-autoround-int4-b70 release assets once.** Same 8 filenames, same manifest, missing for 4 dependent packages. Saving: one upload clears the gap for all 4. Generalizes to any family inheriting closure from a shared manifest.
