# Efficiency audit, 2026-09-15

## Verdict

The lab is fast at the bench, slow at the edges. 797 commits in 7 days,
and `ltx25-b70` produced a bitwise-exact 1.83x sampler speedup from
per-block XPU graph capture (`sampler_speedup: 1.834`,
`data/graph-capture-19-result.json`) — real, but "one process," short of
Rule 3's two-fresh-server bar. The real cost sits where the measuring
stick and publication pipeline both miss it: (1)
`efficiency-audit-metrics.py` "saw" only 4 campaigns because most lanes
now nest `preregistration.json` in a subdirectory its flat
`*-prereg.json` glob can't see, so the busiest lane (`qwen38-27b-b70`,
109 commits) is unmeasured; and (2) 11/27 publication-tier packages
genuinely fail closure, plus a scanner that silently reports every
release asset "missing" without `gh` (as here). Net: faster at
optimizing, not at publishing or self-measurement.

## Top three changes

1. **Match the measuring stick to the naming lanes actually use.** Extend `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` to match `data/*/preregistration.json`, fix campaign-id extraction (empties on that filename today), check `summary.json` for outcome, and sort commits by parsed datetime, not the raw `%cI` string (this window mixes `-04:00`/`+00:00`/`+02:00` offsets). Evidence: 4 counted vs. 11+ new preregistration files. Saving: restores real throughput visibility. Generalizes to any lane using this convention.
2. **Fix two closure-scanner false signals before trusting its counts.** It flags container-build paths as host gaps (`/opt/vllm-src`, built by its own Dockerfile) and silently empties the asset set, not an error, when `gh` fails. Saving: stops false alarms and wasted uploads. Generalizes to any CI/audit environment without `gh`.
3. **Give each active lane a publication-distance line each cycle.** Neither LTX nor Qwen gets one here, though `CURRENT.md` has it: LTX's 1.83x needs a second server; Qwen's FP8 service is qualified but offline pending a NaN-comparison decision. Saving: "how close to done" becomes answerable without re-reading the window.
