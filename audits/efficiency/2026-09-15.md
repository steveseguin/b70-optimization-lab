## Top three changes

1. **Discover campaigns by directory, join prereg notes, normalize outcomes.** Most new `qwen38-27b-b70` dirs have `summary.json` but no `preregistration.json`; some pair with a `notes/*-prereg.md` outside `data/*/`. Their `summary.json` schemas vary (`passed`/`promoted`, nested `closure.status`), not the script's flat `status`/`decision`. Walk `data/*/`, join same-named `notes/*-prereg.md` for start time, normalize outcome keys. Saving: restores throughput, latency, and outcome visibility. Generalizes to any lane mixing conventions.
2. **Fix two closure-scanner false signals.** It flags container-build paths as host gaps and empties the asset set when `gh` fails. Saving: stops false alarms. Generalizes to any `gh`-less CI environment.
3. **Give each active lane a publication-distance line each cycle.** Neither LTX nor Qwen gets one, though it's findable: LTX's 1.86x is qualified but has no `repro/` package; `qwen38-27b-fp8-vllm-tp2-asrock-b70` needs a tested driver/Docker path, recovery guidance, and an independent replay (its own README). Saving: "how close to done" is answerable without re-reading.
