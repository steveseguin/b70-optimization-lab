# Efficiency audit, 2026-09-15

## Verdict

The lab is fast at the bench and slow at the edges. 797 commits landed in
7 days across two active hosts, and `ltx25-b70` alone produced a 2.4x
exact GPU-graph-capture speedup with a clean bitwise-exact chain
(`e512a295`, `2b884736`, `4fd97972`, `3c0a10cb`). But the week's real cost
sits where the fixed measuring stick and the publication pipeline both
miss it: (1) `scripts/efficiency-audit-metrics.py` "saw" only 4 of this
week's campaigns because most lanes now write
`data/<campaign>/preregistration.json` instead of the flat `*-prereg.json`
name its glob expects, so the busiest lane (`qwen38-27b-b70`, 109 commits)
is invisible to it; and (2) 14 of 27 publication-tier packages
(`tools/public-closure-scanner.py`) still have closure gaps — hardcoded
host paths and unreachable image builders, mostly in the flash-next
family — so bench throughput is not converting into portable recipes as
fast as the commit count suggests. Net: faster at optimizing, not yet
faster at publishing or at recovering from infrastructure.

## Rule compliance

1. Census before bisection: no violation signal in window notes.
2. Oracle bound to kernel identity: not verifiable from commit subjects alone.
3. No speed verdict from <2 servers: `rule_signals.single_server_speed_verdicts` empty (low confidence — script saw only 4 campaigns).
4. Whole campaign, one runner: **tension** — Stretch #1 (4 sequential human checkpoints).
5. Fast-fail GPU faults: **compliant** — FP8 restore fault caught by specific signatures, not a timeout.
6. Stop at first passing gate: not applicable (metadata tweak was gate-neutral, not passing).
7. Delegate read-only work while GPUs busy: plausible — claude:codex ratio (493:301) held through the incident window.
8. One lane per host: compliant — `ltx25-b70` (four-B70) and `qwen38-27b-b70` (two-B70) stayed separate.

## Top three changes

1. **Match the measuring stick to the naming lanes actually use.** Extend `scripts/efficiency-audit-metrics.py`'s glob to also match `experiments/*/data/*/preregistration.json` (currently only `*/data/*prereg*.json`). Evidence: 4 campaigns counted vs. at least 11 new preregistration-named files in window (4 flat + 6 `ltx25-b70` subdir + 1 `qwen38-27b-b70` subdir). Saving: restores visibility into most of this week's real throughput for every future audit. Generalizes to any lane using the per-campaign-directory convention, not just this week's two.
2. **Preregister incident-recovery-to-validation as one runner.** Stretch #1's OOM→fault→4-checkpoint chain is exactly what Rule 4 targets. Evidence: `CURRENT.md` Sept14 19:40–Sept15 05:35. Saving: hours per GPU-fault/OOM recovery, since health-check, alloc-diagnostic, campaign and gate could run unattended with one review. Generalizes to any lane resuming after a host incident.
3. **Fix the shared cause of flash-next closure gaps once.** 4 sibling flash-next packages each carry ~80-104 near-identical `host_path_hardcoded` findings from the same container templates. Saving: one script/Dockerfile fix clears most of the 14-package gap count at once instead of per-package patching. Generalizes to any future lane built from the same flash-next template.
