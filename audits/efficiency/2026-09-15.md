## Top three changes

1. **Discover campaigns by directory, and join separate prereg notes.** Most new `qwen38-27b-b70` campaign dirs have `summary.json` but no `preregistration.json`; some (e.g. `prefill-followup`) pair with a `notes/*-prereg.md` outside `data/*/` entirely. Walk `data/*/` for outcome, join same-named `notes/*-prereg.md` for start time. Saving: restores throughput and latency visibility. Generalizes to any lane mixing conventions.
2. **Fix two closure-scanner false signals before trusting its counts.** It flags container-build paths as host gaps and empties the asset set when `gh` fails. Saving: stops false alarms. Generalizes to any CI/audit environment without `gh`.
3. **Give each active lane a publication-distance line each cycle.** Neither LTX nor Qwen gets one, though it's findable: LTX's 1.86x is qualified but has no `repro/` package; `qwen38-27b-fp8-vllm-tp2-asrock-b70` is candidate-tier per its own README, needing a tested driver/Docker path, recovery guidance, and an independent host replay. Saving: "how close to done" is answerable without re-reading.
