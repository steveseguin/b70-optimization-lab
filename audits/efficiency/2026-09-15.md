## Top three changes

1. **Discover campaigns by directory, not preregistration filename.** Most new `qwen38-27b-b70` campaign dirs have `summary.json` but no `preregistration.json` — only 1 of 5 does — so a fixed glob alone would still miss 4 of 5. Walk `data/*/`, accept any of `preregistration.json`/`plan.json`/`summary.json`. Saving: restores real throughput visibility. Generalizes to any lane mixing conventions.
2. **Fix two closure-scanner false signals before trusting its counts.** It flags container-build paths as host gaps and silently empties the asset set when `gh` fails. Saving: stops false alarms. Generalizes to any CI/audit environment without `gh`.
3. **Give each active lane a publication-distance line each cycle.** Neither LTX nor Qwen gets one, though `CURRENT.md` has it: LTX's 1.86x is qualified but has no `repro/` package; Qwen's FP8 service is qualified but offline pending a NaN decision. Saving: "how close to done" is answerable without re-reading.
