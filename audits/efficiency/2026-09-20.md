## Top three changes

1. **Never launch a new GPU campaign on this host after a note declares it
   corrupting data, until the memtest that note demands actually runs.**
   Evidence: packet 83 found byte-level corruption on two boots and named
   memtest86+ "ahead of every software lever"; packets 84-86 launched three
   more campaigns on further boots with no confirmed memtest, leaving even
   packet 84's own "provisional" pass unverified. Saving: stops a week of
   GPU time being spent on results nobody can trust. Generalizes: any lane
   that finds hardware-level corruption needs a hard gate, not a caveat.
2. **Run this audit's metrics scripts against a clean checkout of the
   endpoint commit, after unshallowing — never the audit's own branch.**
   Evidence: this run misread shallow clone `9ad6d4f2` as a squash, then
   reported 665/327/338 commits from its own contaminated branch; direct
   `38214407` gives 660/323/337. Generalizes: every future run risks both.
3. **Redesign campaign discovery in the metrics script — a glob edit alone
   won't fix it.** Evidence: the top-level glob counted 4 of 11 JSON pregs
   added this window; fixing that still mislabels the lane, still can't
   pair a nested preg with its real result, and still misses `*-prereg.md`
   under `notes/`. Generalizes: every lane on these conventions is
   undercounted.
