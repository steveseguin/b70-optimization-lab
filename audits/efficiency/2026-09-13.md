   Only `experiments/*/data/*prereg*.json` (12 ever for
   `qwen38-flash-next-fp8-b70`; 0 for `qwen35-4b-b70`/`qwen35-9b-b70`) paired

   markdown-based lane is undercounted every week.

   report's first draft ran in a clone with four shallow grafts, one inside

   (true diff: 31 files) and inflated the campaign count from 1 to 417.

   Generalizes: any fresh clone reproduces it.

   from CURRENT.md.** Saving: readers hit non-reproducible surfaces now, not
