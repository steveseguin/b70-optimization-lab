line certified and published at 46.85 tok/s (`a6c973c3`), and the whole
INT4/FP8 stack rebased onto stock vLLM v0.29.0 as R304 with three upstream

happened. Read through the notes instead, the week was uneven: an 85.3-hour
stretch of near-silence, then a compressed two-day burst of 85 commits
carrying three days of backlogged experiment notes in one drop.

## Top three changes

1. **Give preregistration/result JSON their own `recorded_utc` field; stop
   trusting git first-commit-time for latency.** Evidence: every one of the
   417 campaigns and 270 results in `/tmp/eff.json` carries the identical
   `prereg_committed`/`result_committed` timestamp `2026-09-08T22:02:52-04:00`
   (the moment of `e754bd02`), because the script derives dates from
   `git log --diff-filter=A` and that commit re-added the whole tree.
   Expected saving: restores the one number showing whether campaigns are
   getting faster; it currently reads 0.0h and hides both progress and
   regressions. Generalizes: any future squash, filter-branch, or migration
   reproduces this exact blindness for every lane, not just this week's.
2. **Commit notes and preregs same-day instead of batching.** Evidence: 30
   notes across four lanes, content-dated 2026-09-09 to 2026-09-11, all first
   entered git in the single `24d7f82e` commit on 2026-09-12T20:13 — the
   85.3h gap above. Expected saving: three days of experimental state were
   invisible to CURRENT.md readers and this audit alike; a fault or a result
   needing review during that window would not have surfaced until the
   catch-up commit. Generalizes to every lane: uncommitted local state is a
   standing blind spot regardless of which model is running.
3. **Close the 11 candidate publication gaps before they reach CURRENT.md.**
   Evidence: `tools/public-closure-scanner.py` flags 11 candidate packages
   with GAPS, e.g. `qwen38-flash-next-fp8-tp4-mtp1-placement-b70-32tps-20260906`
   and `-qsafused-...-20260907` (each ~87-88 `host_path_hardcoded` entries,
   a `missing_release_asset` for `qwen38-flash-next-runtime-stage-2f829747.tar`
   parts, and one `image_without_reachable_builder`), plus
   `qwen38-27b-fp8-vllm-tp2-asrock-b70` (499 files). Expected saving: this is
   a read-only, GPU-free check; running it pre-publish (not only in this
   weekly audit) avoids a rejected publish once GPUs have moved to the next
   lane. Generalizes: the same scanner covers every future candidate package.
