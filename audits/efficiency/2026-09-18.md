## Top three changes

1. **Add `watchdog:.*hard LOCKUP` to the shared GPU-fault fast-fail regex.**
   Evidence: two identical in-server hard lockups 09-15
   (`xe-lockup-incident-01.md`), one undetected since the pattern only
   matches `Fault response|CAT error|engine reset|GPU HANG|GuC.*reset|
   coredump` plus RCU/soft-lockup forms. Saving: faster detection *only*
   for lockups during an active launcher/runner, as in both 09-15 events
   — not the 09-17 idle freeze (zero GPU process running; needs a
   host-level monitor instead). Generalizes to every lane's launcher.
2. **Make the metrics script's prereg discovery recursive; add a
   shallow-clone guard.** Evidence: undercounted campaigns 4 vs ≥11 real
   preregs (7 invisible to the flat glob); the shallow clone separately
   fabricated `commits_total`/`campaigns_total`/latency, reported every
   audit `2026-09-03`–`2026-09-12`, unfixed 6+ runs. Saving: no manual
   re-derivation each week. Generalizes to every future week.
3. **Widen the metrics script's result-file discovery past `*result*.json`.**
   Evidence: line 113 globs only `{stem}*result*.json`;
   `encoder-screen-01/summary.json` and `stability-01/summary.json` never
   match, so those campaigns stay `no-result-yet` forever regardless of
   their fields (found-but-unlabeled scores `unclassified`, not dropped —
   the real loss is files never opened). Saving: recovers real
   latency/outcome data. Generalizes to every `summary.json`-naming lane.
