## Top three changes

1. **Add a host heartbeat/watchdog independent of the journal poller.**
   Evidence: A8 and A57 are both journal-less freezes, and A57's note
   names three more this window. Saving: recovers the lost time per
   freeze. Generalizes: any lane can freeze; a watchdog is infrastructure,
   not a per-model fix.
2. **Gate layer/component bisection on a prior kernel-census run for that
   shape.** Evidence: R64-R116 is the exact pattern rule 1 closed; its
   census took twenty minutes on one card. Saving: the three-day R64-R146
   saga fell to about four hours once census ran first (R151 at 22:47,
   R166 closeout 02:52). Generalizes: the failure signature is
   model-independent.
3. **Fix the outcome classifier/result-matching and make the closure
   scanner offline-safe.** Evidence: three distinct bugs inflate
   `accepted`; 19 phantom closure findings came from a missing `gh`.
   Saving: every future audit stops re-discovering the same false
   positives. Generalizes: any read-only run of these audit inputs is hit.
