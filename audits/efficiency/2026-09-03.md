## Top three changes

1. **Add a host heartbeat/watchdog independent of journal polling, and
   poll the journal live during startup, not postflight-only
   (R148/R152).** Evidence: A8's journal has no coverage of its window; A57's
   covered its window and logged nothing. Saving: recovers lost time per
   freeze. Generalizes: any lane can freeze; a watchdog is infrastructure.
2. **Gate every hypothesis on a prior kernel-census run, not just the
   first.** Evidence: R64-R116 is the pattern rule 1 closed; R152/R154
   later tested fixes before R155's census found the cause.
   Saving: AGENTS.md's rule-1 quote — "settled in an evening" once census
   led. Generalizes: model-independent.
3. **Fix the classifier/result-matching and make the closure scanner
   offline-safe.** Evidence: several bugs inflate `accepted`; 19 phantom
   findings came from a missing `gh`. Saving: future audits stop
   re-discovering the same false positives. Generalizes to any read-only
   audit run.
