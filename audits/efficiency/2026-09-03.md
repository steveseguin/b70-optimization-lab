## Top three changes

1. **Add a host heartbeat/watchdog independent of the per-server journal
   poller.** Evidence: A8 and A57 are both journal-less freezes, and
   A57's note names three more this window. Saving: recovers the lost
   time per undetected freeze. Generalizes: any lane on this rack can
   freeze this way; a watchdog is infrastructure, not a per-model fix.
2. **Gate layer/component bisection on a prior kernel-census run for that
   shape.** Evidence: D8-D14 is the exact pattern rule 1 closed; its
   census step took twenty minutes on one card per `DO-NOT-REPEAT.md`.
   Saving: rule 1's origin story, a three-day saga (R64-R146), fell to
   about four hours once census ran first (R151 at 22:47, R166 closeout
   02:52). Generalizes: the failure signature is model-independent.
3. **Make the closure scanner offline-safe and re-run it on every
   promotion.** Evidence: this run manufactured 19 false findings because
   `gh` isn't installed, and separately the last real scan predates the
   R156 promotion it's cited to validate. Saving: avoids both a phantom
   gap and a false "clean" read. Generalizes to any offline or
   non-publish-triggered scan.
