## Top three changes

1. **Add a host heartbeat/watchdog independent of the per-server journal
   poller.** Evidence: A8 lost ~22.9h to a hard hang with no journal
   writes; `CURRENT.md` records two same-day reboots on 2026-09-02 (08:16,
   18:23 EDT, the second after a Flash-Next A61 kernel soft lockup).
   Saving: recovers most of a day per undetected hang; this window had at
   least two. Generalizes: any lane on this rack can hard-hang; a watchdog
   is infrastructure, not a per-model fix.
2. **Gate layer/component bisection on a prior kernel-census run for that
   shape.** Evidence: d10-d14 (35.5h, 10 arms) repeats the exact pattern
   rule 1 closed; the census that solved it took twenty minutes on one
   card per `DO-NOT-REPEAT.md`. Saving: ~35h collapses to under an hour, as
   R151-R166 then showed. Generalizes: the failure signature (output
   flips with batch shape) is model-independent and will recur.
3. **Close the `qwen38-27b-fp8-vllm-tp2-asrock-b70` publication gap and run
   the closure scanner as a publish-time gate, not only an audit check.**
   Evidence: 19 `missing_release_asset` entries in that package's README,
   build script, and manifest. Saving: avoids a reproduction attempt
   hitting the gap after a fresh clone and GPU cycle. Generalizes: every
   "public chain rebuilt" campaign should be closure-scanned before done.
