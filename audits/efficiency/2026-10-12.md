## Top three changes

1. **Fix campaign outcome classification before trusting any throughput count.**
   `resolution102-closeout/summary.json` and `resolution103-closeout/summary.json` carry none of the four keys
   `result_outcome()` reads (`status`/`decision`/`classification`/`outcome`), so both default to "unclassified"/
   "no-result-yet" despite real recorded throughput; `resolution101c-closeout` is "accepted" only because an
   unrelated `outcome` field happens to contain the substring "...passed." The real result file
   (`resolution-campaign-result.json`, `"passed": true`) never joins its own preregistration, because
   `campaign_slug()` strips only a trailing `-result` token, leaving `resolution-campaign` instead of
   `resolution102`. Last week's change 3, still open and worse (48/50 vs. 18/21 unclassified). Saving: real
   accept/reject counts. Generalizes: any lane whose result JSON skips those keys, or embeds extra words before
   `-result`, vanishes from future audits.
2. **Make the infra-event scan recursive.** It globs only `experiments/*/notes/*.md` and
   `experiments/*/data/*result*.json`, one level deep. `own-xpu-runtime` keeps fault evidence under
   `stage2/packetNN/evidence/**`, deeper than that; its two real faults this week, including the one needing an
   owner reboot call, produced zero entries among the 30 files counted. Saving: the infra-tax section is blind
   to the highest-fault-risk lane. Generalizes: any lane with its own evidence layout stays invisible until the
   glob is widened.
3. **Squash stage receipts to one commit per arm.** 246 of 1093 commits end in "receipts" (279 of 481 last
   week — no change since proposed). Saving: the largest source of commit noise this audit reads through, two
   weeks running. Generalizes beyond LTX: the context sub-lane (`Context:`/`keeper`/`ctxkeeper`, ~76 commits)
   shows the same habit.
