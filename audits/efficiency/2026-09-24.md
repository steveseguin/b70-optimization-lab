## Top three changes

1. **Check `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` before trusting any
   `--since` window, in this script and any future audit.** A shallow
   clone silently truncates history at a commit that looks like a root;
   this cost the review cycle before an independent count
   (192, not 50) forced `git fetch --unshallow`. Generalizes to any agent
   reasoning over "the last N days" in a fresh clone.
2. **Write one fault-classification runbook the human follows for LTX,
   not an automated runner** — the stability constraints ban
   host-controlled wrappers outright. Packets 77–90 each cost a human
   investigation; a decision tree cuts that with zero automation.
3. **Verify a promotion attestation's cited evidence paths, not just its
   prose.** R312d-c's attestation claimed a same-image oracle its
   `performance_receipts` paths don't support (above); apply this check to
   every future attestation.
