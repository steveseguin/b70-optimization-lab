## Top three changes

1. **Check `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` before trusting any
   `--since` window.** A shallow clone truncates
   history at a plausible-looking root; this cost a review cycle until
   an outside count (192, not 50) forced `git fetch --unshallow`.
   Generalizes to any agent reasoning over history in a fresh clone.
2. **Write one fault-classification runbook the human follows for
   LTX** — stability constraints ban host-controlled wrappers. Packets
   77–90 each cost a human investigation; a tree cuts that. Generalizes
   to any lane banning automated recovery.
3. **Verify a promotion attestation's cited evidence paths, not just its
   prose.** R312d-c claimed a same-image oracle its `performance_receipts`
   paths don't support (above); catches unqualified promotions pre-ship,
   for future attestations.
