## Top three changes

1. **Check `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` before trusting any
   `--since` window, in this or future audits.** A shallow clone
   truncates history at a commit that looks like a root; this cost the
   review cycle before an independent count (192, not 50) forced
   `git fetch --unshallow`. Generalizes to any agent reasoning over "the
   last N days" in a fresh clone.
2. **Write one fault-classification runbook the human follows for LTX,
   not an automated runner** — stability constraints ban host-controlled
   wrappers. Packets 77–90 cost a human investigation each; a decision
   tree cuts that, unautomated.
3. **Verify a promotion attestation's cited evidence paths, not just its
   prose.** R312d-c claimed a same-image oracle its `performance_receipts`
   paths don't support (above); catches an unqualified promotion before
   it ships. Applies to every future attestation.
