## Top three changes

1. **Give every "waiting on reboot/decision" `CURRENT.md` entry an expiry check-in.**
   Evidence: `f6f5cdbd` recorded the four-B70 halt on 2026-09-21; nine days and zero
   commits later nothing has re-surfaced it. Expected saving: bounds blocked-lane idle
   time, which is currently unbounded, to the check-in interval instead of "until
   someone happens to look." Generalizes because every lane will keep hitting
   fault-halts under the no-auto-restart policy in AGENTS.md, and each one needs the
   same nudge rather than a bespoke one per lane.

2. **Keep the efficiency audit itself on a fixed cadence with no silent gaps.**
   Evidence: `audits/efficiency/` has reports for 2026-09-03 through 2026-09-12, then
   none until this one on 2026-09-30 — an 18-day blind spot in the one process built to
   catch a week like this. Expected saving: a stall like the current one would surface
   on the first missed week instead of the third. Generalizes to any future lane or
   host, since the audit is host- and lane-agnostic by design.

3. **Use the idle-GPU window to advance the queued decisions with read-only work,
   per the existing Cross-Agent Delegation rule.** Evidence: `CURRENT.md`'s two-B70
   section lists three unresolved DECISION items since 2026-09-18/19 (reboot, resume
   script, 62 GB deletion), and AGENTS.md rule 7 already authorizes delegating
   "recipe audits, preregistration drafting, and result summarization" through
   `codex exec --sandbox read-only` without waiting for a GPU; no commit in the window
   shows this happening. Expected saving: when the human answers the pending
   decisions, the next campaign launches immediately instead of restarting planning
   from scratch. Generalizes to every future fault-halt or user-decision block, on
   either host.
