## Top three changes

1. **Extend campaign/arm discovery to the packet-note convention.** Evidence: 2 `*prereg*.md` vs. 592 `*result*`
   files this window, 102,855 own-xpu-runtime path entries, yet `campaigns_total`=1,
   `prereg_to_result_latency.n`=0. Saving: restores throughput/latency/outcome reporting for the week's largest
   lane. Generalizes: any lane with its own numbering scheme (LTX already, now own-xpu-runtime) is otherwise
   invisible to this auditor by default.
2. **Add a staleness flag for open preregistrations.** Evidence: `2026-10-16-fp8-piece-size-prereg.md` (10-17
   00:54) has no result file in the window. Saving: a fully designed, qualified campaign (predicted 10%+ prefill win) stops sitting idle
   by accident. Generalizes: Rule 4 ("never stop optimizing") means preregistrations will keep outpacing closures;
   a cheap check scales with that habit.
3. **Pre-flight memory check before long unattended campaigns on the 15 GiB host.** Evidence: six host-memory-guard
   kills (18:31, 21:03, 21:17 on 10-17; 00:53, 02:45 on 10-18) in
   `2026-10-17-hostile-history-poison.md`, each causing a GPU CAT error/GT reset and a recovery probe, before the
   root cause (client memory) was fixed. Saving: one peak-RSS check before launch catches the margin problem once
   instead of six times. Generalizes: any long campaign on that host is one client-side growth away from the same
   kill-fault cycle.
