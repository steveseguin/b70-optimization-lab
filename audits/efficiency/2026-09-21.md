## Top three changes

1. **Give the LTX runner continuous journal-liveness polling during arms, not
   just signature matching.** `run-campaign-82.sh` only polls (for fault
   signatures) before launch. Evidence: in `d8125830` the journal produced no
   `Fault`/CAT/reset/coredump line — it just stopped advancing at 17:35:01 while
   writes continued to 17:36:30 (storage-first hang), so a signature poll alone
   wouldn't catch it; only a "no new entries for N seconds" timeout would.
   Saving: catches a silent wedge in seconds, not by a human finding it.
   Generalizes: any unattended runner needs a liveness timeout, not just
   error-signature matching.
2. **Give `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` a shallow-clone guard, and teach it the
   nested campaign schema — glob plus parser.** Evidence: shallow checkout
   (~50 commits) first reported `campaigns_total: 421`, 0.0h median latency — a
   shallow-boundary artifact. Deepened, it fell to 0: the glob only matches flat
   `data/*prereg*.json`, and LTX has nested `data/<campaign>/preregistration.json`
   since 09-13. Widening the glob isn't enough — `lane = prereg.parts[-3]` and
   the sibling `*result*.json` search both assume flat depth. Named 09-12, fix
   proposed 09-09, still unfixed. Saving: restores every signal this audit exists
   to produce. Generalizes to every future lane.
3. **Fix `public-closure-scanner.py`'s `release_assets()`, `PATH_RE`, and
   `CONTAINER_PATHS`.** Evidence: `gh` is absent, so its bare `except: set()`
   produces 75 false `missing_release_asset` findings; `PATH_RE` truncates paths
   within 46 `missing_path` findings; `CONTAINER_PATHS` misses lane-specific
   image dirs (`/opt/vllm-src`, `CCL_KERNEL_PATH`), so ≥130 of 794
   `host_path_hardcoded` hits are container-internal, not real gaps. Saving:
   separates real gaps from noise. Generalizes to every future scan.
