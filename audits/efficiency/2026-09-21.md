## Top three changes

1. **Give the LTX runner continuous kernel-journal polling during arms.**
   AGENTS.md already requires it (fast-fail on the first `Fault response`);
   `run-campaign-82.sh` only polls before launch. Evidence: in `d8125830` the
   journal died 17:35:01 while writes continued to 17:36:30 — a human found the
   host wedged, not a poll. Saving: catches a fault in seconds instead of
   needing a human to notice a silent wedge. Generalizes: any unattended runner
   needs fault detection for its whole run, not just launch.
2. **Give `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` a shallow-clone guard, and teach it the
   nested campaign schema — glob plus parser.** Evidence: this run's checkout was
   shallow (~50 commits); the first pass reported `campaigns_total: 421`, 0.0h
   median latency — a shallow-boundary artifact. Deepened, it fell to 0: the
   glob only matches flat `data/*prereg*.json`, and LTX has nested
   `data/<campaign>/preregistration.json` since 09-13. Widening the glob alone
   isn't enough — `lane = prereg.parts[-3]` and the sibling `*result*.json`
   search both assume flat depth. Named 09-12, fix proposed 09-09, still
   unfixed. Saving: restores every signal this audit exists to produce.
   Generalizes to every future lane.
3. **Fix `public-closure-scanner.py`'s `release_assets()`, `PATH_RE`, and
   `CONTAINER_PATHS`.** Evidence: `gh` is absent, so its bare `except: set()`
   produces 75 false `missing_release_asset` findings; `PATH_RE` truncates paths
   within 46 `missing_path` findings (09-12's bug); `CONTAINER_PATHS` misses
   lane-specific image dirs like `/opt/vllm-src`, so 130 of 794
   `host_path_hardcoded` hits are Dockerfile-internal, not real gaps. Saving:
   separates real gaps from noise. Generalizes to every future scan.
