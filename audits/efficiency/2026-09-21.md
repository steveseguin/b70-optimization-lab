## Top three changes

1. **Give the LTX runner a stall detector on its own receipt/commit path, paired
   with incremental dispatch or a queue-cancel call.** `run-throughput-fixtures.py`
   submits all 120 endurance prompts upfront, then only polls history — so
   detecting a stall alerts sooner but can't itself stop in-flight server work
   without one of those two. Evidence: in `d8125830` the journal produced no
   `Fault`/CAT/reset/coredump line — it just stopped advancing at 17:35:01 while
   the GPU pipeline kept completing clips; raw journal silence isn't a fault
   either — a healthy postflight journal is empty too
   (`guarded-v2-native-attempt-01.md`). The real signature is receipts/commits
   not landing while work is reported ongoing. Saving: turns an hours-later
   human discovery into a seconds-later alert. Generalizes: any unattended
   runner needs a liveness signal on its own output, and a way to actually stop
   dispatched work, not just detect it.
2. **Give `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` a shallow-clone guard, and teach it the
   nested campaign schema.** Evidence: shallow checkout (~50 commits) first
   reported `campaigns_total: 421`, 0.0h median latency — a shallow-boundary
   artifact. Deepened, it fell to 0: the glob only matches flat
   `data/*prereg*.json`, and LTX has nested `data/<campaign>/preregistration.json`
   since 09-13; `lane = prereg.parts[-3]` assumes flat depth too. Named 09-12,
   fix proposed 09-09, still unfixed. Saving: restores every signal this audit
   exists to produce. Generalizes to every future lane.
3. **Fix `public-closure-scanner.py`'s `release_assets()`, `PATH_RE`, and
   `CONTAINER_PATHS`.** Evidence: `gh` is absent, so its bare `except: set()`
   produces 75 false `missing_release_asset` findings; `PATH_RE` truncates paths
   in 46 `missing_path` findings; `CONTAINER_PATHS` misses `/opt/vllm-src`,
   `CCL_KERNEL_PATH`, so ≥130 of 794 `host_path_hardcoded` hits are
   container-internal. Saving: separates real gaps from noise. Generalizes to
   every future scan.
