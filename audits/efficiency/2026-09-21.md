## Top three changes

1. **Give the LTX runner an independent stall watchdog on its receipt/commit
   path, paired with incremental dispatch or a queue-cancel call.**
   `run-throughput-fixtures.py` submits all 120 endurance prompts upfront, then
   polls `/history` through a call that can itself block ~300s × 30 retries
   (`call()`) — a detector on that path inherits its blocking, so it must run
   independently. Evidence: in `d8125830` the journal produced no
   `Fault`/CAT/reset/coredump line — it just stopped advancing at 17:35:01 while
   the GPU pipeline kept completing clips; raw silence isn't a fault either — a
   healthy postflight journal is empty too (`guarded-v2-native-attempt-01.md`).
   Saving: turns an hours-later human discovery into a bounded, fast alert.
   Generalizes: any unattended runner needs a watchdog independent of its own
   blocking calls, and a way to stop dispatched work, not just detect it.
2. **Give `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` a shallow-clone guard, and teach it the
   nested campaign schema.** Evidence: shallow checkout first reported
   `campaigns_total: 421`, 0.0h median latency — a shallow-boundary artifact.
   Deepened, it fell to 0: the glob only matches flat `data/*prereg*.json`,
   LTX has nested `data/<campaign>/preregistration.json` since 09-13, and
   `lane = prereg.parts[-3]` assumes flat depth too. Named 09-12, fix proposed
   09-09, still unfixed. Saving: restores every signal this audit exists to
   produce. Generalizes to every future lane.
3. **Fix `public-closure-scanner.py`'s `release_assets()`, `PATH_RE`, and
   `CONTAINER_PATHS`.** Evidence: `gh` is absent, so `except: set()` never
   returns a true negative — all 75 `missing_release_asset` findings are
   unreliable, not provably false (≥6, `mtp3-b70`, confirmed false via its own
   `publication-readback.json` hash match; rest unverified). `PATH_RE`
   truncates paths in 46 `missing_path` findings; `CONTAINER_PATHS` misses
   `/opt/vllm-src`, `CCL_KERNEL_PATH`, so ≥130 of 794 `host_path_hardcoded`
   hits are container-internal. Saving: separates real gaps from noise instead
   of guessing. Generalizes to every future scan.
