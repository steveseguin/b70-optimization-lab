## Top three changes

1. **Give the LTX runner a stall detector on its own receipt/commit path during
   arms, alongside its pre-launch signature poll.** `run-campaign-82.sh` only
   polls for fault signatures before launch. Evidence: in `d8125830` the journal
   produced no `Fault`/CAT/reset/coredump line — it just stopped advancing at
   17:35:01 while the GPU pipeline kept completing clips, so a signature poll
   alone wouldn't catch it; but raw journal silence isn't a fault either — a
   healthy postflight journal is empty too (`guarded-v2-native-attempt-01.md`).
   The real signature is receipts/commits not landing while work is reported
   ongoing. Saving: catches a silent wedge in seconds without false-aborting
   healthy arms. Generalizes: any unattended runner needs progress-on-its-own-
   output as its liveness signal, not journal silence alone.
2. **Give `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` a shallow-clone guard, and teach it the
   nested campaign schema — glob plus parser.** Evidence: shallow checkout
   (~50 commits) first reported `campaigns_total: 421`, 0.0h median latency — a
   shallow-boundary artifact. Deepened, it fell to 0: the glob only matches flat
   `data/*prereg*.json`, and LTX has nested `data/<campaign>/preregistration.json`
   since 09-13; widening it isn't enough, since `lane = prereg.parts[-3]` assumes
   flat depth too. Named 09-12, fix proposed 09-09, still unfixed. Saving:
   restores every signal this audit exists to produce. Generalizes to every
   future lane.
3. **Fix `public-closure-scanner.py`'s `release_assets()`, `PATH_RE`, and
   `CONTAINER_PATHS`.** Evidence: `gh` is absent, so its bare `except: set()`
   produces 75 false `missing_release_asset` findings; `PATH_RE` truncates paths
   within 46 `missing_path` findings; `CONTAINER_PATHS` misses `/opt/vllm-src`,
   `CCL_KERNEL_PATH`, so ≥130 of 794 `host_path_hardcoded` hits are
   container-internal, not real gaps. Saving: separates real gaps from noise.
   Generalizes to every future scan.
