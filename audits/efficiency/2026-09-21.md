## Top three changes

1. **Add a fault-count circuit breaker to the LTX runner:** after 2 freeze/fault
   incidents on one boot, halt new packets and run the cheap hardware check first.
   Evidence: eleven freeze incidents (`a1c9afdf`→`164f585a`) were chased through
   firmware, kernel, and runtime-PM theories before a memtester ran (`ebcd56cd`), by
   which point the boot was already burned. Saving: days of hypothesis churn
   (09-15–09-20) replaced by an early hardware signal. Generalizes: any lane/host
   with repeated faults should get a cheap health check before more bisection.
2. **Give `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` a shallow-clone guard, and widen its
   campaign glob to also match `data/*/preregistration.json`.** Evidence: this run's
   fresh checkout was shallow (~50 commits, ~33h real history); the first pass
   reported `campaigns_total: 421`, 0.0h median latency — an artifact of the shallow
   boundary commit (`/tmp/eff.json`). Deepened, `campaigns_total` fell to 0, because
   the glob only matches flat `data/*prereg*.json` and LTX has nested
   `data/<campaign>/preregistration.json` since 09-13 — blind to the lane behind 301
   of this week's commits. The shallow-clone half was named 09-12, fix proposed
   09-09, still unfixed. Saving: restores every rule-compliance and latency signal
   this audit exists to produce. Generalizes to every future lane and run.
3. **Fix `public-closure-scanner.py`'s `release_assets()` and `PATH_RE`.** Evidence:
   `gh` is absent here, so its bare `except: set()` turns "can't check" into "0
   assets exist," producing 10 `missing_release_asset` false findings; `PATH_RE`
   still truncates real paths (`repro/qwen35-`, `.../onednn-qwen38-w4a16-`), the bug
   09-12 diagnosed. Saving: turns "12/28 candidates have gaps" from mostly-noise
   into a trustworthy signal. Generalizes to every future scan.
