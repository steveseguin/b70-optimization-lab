1. **Give LTX (and any non-prereg lane) a recognized campaign-outcome
   record.** Evidence: `campaigns_total: 0` this week despite 76 real LTX
   commits and an accepted qwen38-27b result, because none use the prereg/

   audit's own clone was shallow, hiding 55 real commits and inflating a

   `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository` and unshallow or fail loud first.

   "Codex Agent".** `author_class()` overrides the real author on any
   `Co-Authored-By: Claude` trailer. The real split lets an audit judge
   whether `AGENTS.md`'s Cross-Agent Delegation is actually happening.
