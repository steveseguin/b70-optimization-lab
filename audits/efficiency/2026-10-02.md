## Top three changes

1. **Require a committed "lane idle" note the moment an active lane goes
   >24h without a commit, stating the reason.** Evidence: the queue in
   `2026-09-21-capacity-budget-24fps.md` (89→90→91→92) was published, then
   silently abandoned for 11 days with nothing in git distinguishing
   "paused for a reason" from "dropped." Saving: removes note-archaeology
   for whoever resumes any lane that goes quiet. Generalizes to every
   lane/host — a published queue with no closing note is a structural
   failure mode, not an LTX-specific one.
2. **Treat repeated same-day hardware faults as their own gate before the
   next packet launches.** Evidence: five faults in one day (`58caa62c`
   through `d8125830`) on the four-B70 host, each costing a reboot cycle,
   before the queue even reached packet 91. Rule 5 (fast-fail) caught each
   quickly, but no committed gate forces a hardware review (beyond the
   memtest already run) after N faults in a day. Saving: avoids spending a
   full packet's worth of agent+GPU time rediscovering the same fault
   class. Generalizes to any host with recurring faults.
3. **Extend `efficiency-audit-metrics.py` to report days-since-last-commit,
   not just in-window activity.** Evidence: this run's `commits_total: 0`
   is indistinguishable from "a quiet, healthy week" without the manual
   `git log` cross-check that found the true 11-day gap. Saving: the next
   audit gets the idle signal from one script run. Generalizes to every
   future window, including empty ones.
