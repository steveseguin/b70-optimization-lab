# Community Field Reports

This directory preserves useful measurements and operational observations
reported by community members without presenting them as reference-lab
results, runnable recipes, or project recommendations.

Every report here is `community-reported`. A maintainer may check arithmetic,
links, internal consistency, or documentation safety, but those checks do not
verify the benchmark. The report must state what was and was not checked.

Field reports contain documentation only. Runnable commands, launchers,
patches, configurations, downloaded resources, and local validation artifacts
belong in the appropriate normal community entry instead. A field report does
not move directly into `repro/` or `results/`; reproduction produces a
separate, fully identified result packet.
