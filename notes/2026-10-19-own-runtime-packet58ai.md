# Packet58ai: one-card miss census, prepared without queueing

The new packet copies packet58ad and keeps its exact model, arithmetic,
tile16, HC fusion, maximum cache and overlap settings. It separates routed
cache misses from later cache fills. The current record stays 30.396 tok/s.

The first milestone retains the record packet and private native source
closure. It adds ordinary event measurement of the unchanged mixed kernels
and a separate resident/miss partition diagnostic. The latter changes
scheduling, so its cost is reported separately and controls measure overhead.
No model data, GPU, service, live queue or host setting was touched.

Preparation checks, final readiness and any remaining blockers will be
recorded below before the final marker is written. Device output equality,
acceptance identity and actual costs remain pending device execution.

The native builds pass. Both compilers pass 24 exact resident/miss partition
cases twice, including invalid-route handling. Two miniature five-view repeats
match the original 58ad tokens and retained layer bytes; a wrong oracle refuses
before the next view. The active 33 Python tests pass. All three real entry
paths reach intercepted native execution with the real closed environment and
screen-packet58ai-census-A unit. Shared native loader and smoke/bracket report
rehearsals pass. The initial report rehearsal failure is retained: the fixture
needed its explicit parser signature, and census analysis needed its own test
boundary separate from the shared standard-summary adapter.

The packet measures both the original mixed schedule and explicitly changed
resident/miss partitions. Its conservative saving ceiling erases whole
miss-containing original kernels, including unavoidable arithmetic; the
partition service is a separate diagnostic. The reader never presents their
difference as measured memory-stall time. It retains residency differences,
reports both warm-control overhead comparisons and blocks rank-4 decisions
when the original-schedule instrument or control drift fails its gate.

The exact model allocation plan is inherited by hash. No full-shape/model
reads were made during the packet58ag/58ah holds. The only new host buffer is
16 MiB within the existing 32 MiB allowance; device allocations are unchanged.

The final checks also reject unbound census logs and another in-flight job
even when its queue has become empty. The shared entry tool's original report
is retained unchanged, with a CPU-only wrapper for this packet's seal format.
The first temporary plan caught that missing wrapper field before any launch.

Root storage remained above 30 GB. The inherited K1 write allowance briefly
blocked rehearsal. Each unchanged complete record K1 directory contains about
4.06 MB; the new 32 MiB allowance for each is over eight times that amount.
The census keeps its full GiB allowance, and the 30 GB floor stays enforced.
The new build occupies 66,354,729 bytes. Peak compiler RSS was about 744 MB;
the last entry/test rehearsal peaked around 36 MB, below the 4 GB preparation cap.

The retained checks and temporary plan are linked from the
[packet README](../experiments/own-xpu-runtime/stage2/packet58ai-miss-census/README.md).
The final marker binds the prepared commit, proposal and seal. No work was
queued. The coordinator must wait for both two-card queues and other in-flight
jobs to clear, then repeat live admission. Device output/acceptance equality,
instrumentation overhead, miss cost and the threshold decision remain pending;
the CPU checks do not claim a model-device pass.

The final temporary lane-A queue plan passes, with 625 immutable pins across
25 manifests. The shared planner reports its own 25 GB floor; this packet's
actual launch path separately enforces the stricter 30 GB floor after reserving
all 1088 MiB of planned writes.

After the successful entry probes, packet58ah loaded a model. A later repeat
correctly refused host admission with about 71.1 GB available against 99.0 GB
required. That refusal is retained separately. Every source hash from the
earlier passing live-admission probes still matches; no memory observation was
substituted to make a probe pass. Readiness means prepared for the coordinator,
conditional on fresh live admission after the other work clears.

the inherited whole-job watchdog remains. The real analyzer passes complete
