Which model should I try?

Speeds are in tok/s: a token is about ¾ of a word, so 100 tok/s is roughly 75 words a second. The first three speed columns show how fast the model writes answers. Prefill shows how fast it reads your prompt: 512 tokens, one user. “32K input” means writing after a long prompt; “Many users” is the total for everyone. Higher numbers are faster.
