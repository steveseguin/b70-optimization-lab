Start here

  • One B70: the community cookbook — pinned vLLM XPU image, Qwen3.6/Qwen3.8 launchers, Windows 11 kits. Or a single-card llama.cpp lane like Gemma-4-26B.
  • Two to four B70s: TP2/TP4 recipes in the results index and model recipes — MiniMax INT4 TP4, Laguna INT4 + DFlash, DeepSeek-V4 FP4/FP8, Qwen3.6 dense TP2.
  • Optimizing a new model: the model optimization guide is the playbook; the timeline shows how existing lanes got fast.
  • AI agents: read llms.txt for the machine-readable site map, then AGENTS.md.

Contribute

  • Record-setting results are not required — careful negative results and quality checks are valuable too.
  • Bring the evidence: hardware, software pins, model revision, exact command, metric definition, result JSON/logs. Full checklist in CONTRIBUTING.md.
  • Runnable work lands in community/ with a filled STATUS.md; measurements alone go to field-reports. PRs against steveseguin/b70-optimization-lab.
  • Entries enter as community-reported and are promoted only after reference-lab reproduction — see the eight-step validation. Negative results are recorded as evidence, not deleted.
