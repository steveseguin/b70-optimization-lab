What you need

  • The card: a PC with at least one Intel Arc Pro B70 graphics card (32 GB). One card runs most models on this page; the biggest are split across 2–4 cards working together.
  • One card: follow our step-by-step Gemma-4-26B guide (125 tok/s, checked in this lab). More single-card setups are indexed in our model recipes.
  • Two to four cards: complete recipes in the results index — MiniMax INT4, Laguna-S + DFlash, DeepSeek-V4 FP4/FP8, Qwen3.8 dense split across 2 cards.
  • Promoted guides are complete: pinned software versions, exact commands, and a proof folder with logs and quality checks. Research rows explicitly identify unresolved gates or unavailable artifacts.
  • Making a new model fast: the optimization guide is the playbook; the timeline shows how each setup improved.
  • Models we are preparing next: our model intake queue records exact revisions, sizes, checksums, B70 fit, and why each download is worth keeping. Popularity starts an investigation; it does not make a model or outside performance claim verified.
  • Docker and Windows: digest-pinned Linux packages come first; Windows packets stay preview-only until they are replayed on a real Windows B70 machine. Follow the packaging roadmap.
  • For AI assistants: llms.txt is a machine-readable map of this site, then AGENTS.md.

Contribute

  • Record-setting results are not required — careful negative results and quality checks are valuable too.
  • Bring the evidence: hardware, software versions, model revision, exact command, how you measured, result files. Full checklist in CONTRIBUTING.md.
  • Runnable setups land in community/ with a filled STATUS.md; measurements alone go to field-reports. Propose changes on GitHub.
  • Entries enter as community-reported and are promoted only after we reproduce them — see the eight-step validation. Negative results are recorded as evidence, not deleted.
  • Tracked claims keep the exact recipe identity, submitter, evidence, and dated lab outcome in the claims registry. Contributor credit stays attached to concrete work; lab measurements stay attached to lab lanes.
  • When a contributed patch survives a matched quality and performance test, its author and exact patch identity remain attached to the promoted result. A model tip or outside report is acknowledged as a lead without reassigning the lab's existing recipes or optimization history.
