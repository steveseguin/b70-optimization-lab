Community optimization

Made a model faster?

Submit the patch, recipe, measurement, correction, or model lead. We review contributions manually and reproduce performance claims when the evidence and hardware allow. When your concrete contribution produces a verified improvement, we publish the measured boost and keep your name attached everywhere the work is used. Useful negative results receive acknowledgement too.
